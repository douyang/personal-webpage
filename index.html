About

I am a cardiologist and researcher in the Division of Research at Kaiser Permanente Northern California and an adjunct Assistant Professor at the Smidt Heart Institute at Cedars-Sinai. I majored in statistics at Rice University, obtained my MD at UCSF, and received post-graduate medical education in internal medicine, cardiology, and a postdoc in computer science and biomedical data science at Stanford University.

Research

Our mission is to improve cardiovascular diagnosis and disease detection using computer vision and artificial intelligence. We focus on three pillars: AI model development (precision phenotyping), AI model understanding (interpretable medical AI), and AI model deployment (clinical trials). Our work has been published in Nature, Nature Medicine, NEJM AI, Circulation, JAMA Cardiology, and the European Heart Journal. We conducted the first blinded, randomized trial of AI in cardiology, and our echocardiography models are now FDA-cleared and deployed in clinical care.

The best part of the job are the people I get to work with. I have the good fortune of working with a great group of motivated, talented, and creative individuals from a wide range of backgrounds. Our lab members bring expertise from a wide range of backgrounds ranging from physics, mechanical engineering, data science, math, and computer science to cardiology, anesthesia, and internal medicine.

Clinical Trials

We run prospective trials to test whether AI actually improves cardiovascular care — from the first blinded randomized trial of AI in cardiology to health-system deployment studies.

  • AI ECHO INSIGHT — Kaiser Permanente AI echocardiogram interpretation for systematic integration and guided healthcare transformation. Co-Principal Investigator. NCT07229300 (2025–present).
  • ECHONET-LVH-RCT — Blinded, randomized controlled trial of AI-guided precision assessment of wall thickness. Principal Investigator. NCT05791227 (2025–present).
  • ECHONET-SCREENING — AI-guided echocardiographic screening of rare diseases. Co-Principal Investigator. NCT06664866 (2024–present).
  • ECHONET-RCT — Blinded, randomized controlled trial of sonographer vs. AI assessment of cardiac function. Principal Investigator. NCT05140642 (2022; published in Nature).
  • SCOUT ECHO-AI — Screening cardiometabolic opportunities using transformative echocardiography AI. Principal Investigator. NCT07216859 (planned 2028).

InVision

I am a co-founder of InVision Medical Technology, a Y Combinator–backed company building FDA-cleared AI for echocardiography — the path by which our research models reach routine clinical care.

  • Precision LVEF — FDA 510(k) cleared (2024, K232331); AI ejection-fraction measurement validated in a blinded, randomized trial.
  • Precision Cardiac Amyloid — FDA 510(k) cleared (2025, K243866), with FDA Breakthrough Device designation (2024).
  • Medicare reimbursement — CMS New Technology Add-on Payment for Precision Cardiac Amyloid, up to $2,275 per inpatient stay, effective October 2026.
  • Precision Cirrhosis — FDA Breakthrough Device designation for AI detection of end-stage liver disease from echocardiography.
