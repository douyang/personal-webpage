David Ouyang

Cardiologist // AI Researcher // Echocardiography // Machine Learning

Hey there! I’m a cardiologist and researcher studying echocardiography, machine learning, and all things cardiac. My lab builds computer vision and AI systems to improve cardiovascular diagnosis — from model development and interpretability to clinical trial deployment.

I'm interested in the intersection of clinical care and information systems, with a focus on passive data generated in the setting of routine care. The electronic medical record and imaging data provides the ground truth for in-depth statistical learning (ie. machine learning).

I majored in statistics at Rice University, went to medical school at UCSF, and trained in internal medicine and cardiology at Stanford. I now run the Ouyang Lab at the Smidt Heart Institute, Cedars-Sinai.

Selected Publications

ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE + ECHOCARDIOGRAPHY + CARDIOVASCULAR MEDICINE

A selection of recent work. For a complete and up-to-date list, see my Google Scholar profile.

  • Comprehensive echocardiogram evaluation with view-primed vision–language AI (EchoPrime) Vukadinovic M, et al. · Nature · 2025 A multi-video, view-informed vision–language foundation model trained on over 12 million video–report pairs for comprehensive echocardiography interpretation.
  • Artificial Intelligence Automation of Echocardiographic Measurements (EchoNet-Measurements) Sahashi Y, et al. · JACC · 2025 Open-source deep learning segmentation models that automatically quantify 18 anatomic and Doppler measurements from echocardiograms.
  • Artificial intelligence prediction of age from echocardiography as a marker for cardiovascular disease npj Digital Medicine · 2025 A deep learning model estimating biological age from echocardiogram videos, with the age gap serving as a marker of cardiovascular risk.
  • Blinded, randomized trial of sonographer versus AI cardiac function assessment (EchoNet-RCT) Nature · 2023 The first blinded, randomized controlled trial of AI in cardiology, comparing AI and sonographer initial assessment of left ventricular ejection fraction.
  • Video-based AI for beat-to-beat assessment of cardiac function (EchoNet-Dynamic) Nature · 2020 A deep learning model for beat-to-beat assessment of cardiac function from echocardiogram videos, released with a large open dataset.
